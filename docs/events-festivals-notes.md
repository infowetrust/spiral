# Festival dates and research notes

Researched October 2, 2026. Scope: September 27, 2016 through September 26, 2026, inclusive. Dates are local San Francisco calendar dates; both endpoints are inclusive. Records are chronological. `id` identifies the festival series, not a unique occurrence. No dates were generated from recurrence rules.

## Status and scope

- `held` means an in-person edition of the requested festival. Carnaval records cover the full Saturday-Sunday festival, not just Sunday's parade. Setup, teardown, street-closure windows, king/queen competitions, night shows, and unrelated concerts are excluded.
- `cancelled` dates are documented planned dates, not days when a festival happened. Consumers plotting actual attendance or outdoor activity must filter to `status === "held"`.
- `virtual` dates are actual replacement broadcast dates. These are not in-person festival days. A virtual replacement and its canceled original weekend can both appear under the same festival ID.
- Outside Lands 2021 uses October 29-31, not its earlier August 6-8 plan. The superseded plan is not a separate cancellation record because that edition was postponed and held.
- Outside Lands 2020 has separate canceled August 7-9 and virtual August 28-29 records. The [organizer history](https://sfoutsidelands.com/festival-history/) confirms the in-person hiatus and Inside Lands replacement; [co-organizer Another Planet](https://apeconcerts.com/events/inside-lands-8-29/) supplies the broadcast dates.
- Carnaval 2020's May 23-24 planned street festival was initially postponed, and a [May 24 virtual parade and festival](https://sf.funcheap.com/city-guide/carnaval-sf-live-stream-virtual-festival-24th/) replaced the public celebration. `cancelled` describes the nonoccurrence of the original weekend, not an assertion that the first announcement used that word. [The Chronicle's 2020 guide](https://www.sfchronicle.com/entertainment/article/memorial-day-weekend-2020-how-to-celebrate-21188764.php) independently identifies the online May 24 event.
- Hardly Strictly 2020 uses October 3 only: its [organizer archive](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2020/) dates the premiere of the three-hour broadcast. Hardly Strictly 2021 uses the October 1-3 livestream dates from the [festival publicist's release](https://www.gratefulweb.com/articles/hardly-strictly-bluegrass-october-1-3-livestream/), corroborated as an online replacement by the [organizer archive](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2021/). Neither is `held`.

## Omissions

- Carnaval 2021: no traditional full festival record. A smaller in-person resource fair and celebration took place May 29-30, with music and dance, but it replaced the traditional festival and parade. The [organizer-submitted event listing](https://sf.funcheap.com/carnaval-sf-mini-festival-resource-fair-celebration-may-29-30/) explicitly dates both days. [Contemporaneous reporting](https://www.sfchronicle.com/local/article/SF-Carnaval-s-festival-goes-on-in-a-16212889.php) distinguishes it from the traditional event. This narrower replacement is deliberately omitted rather than marking the requested full festival `held`. No original full-festival date range was independently verified, so no guessed `cancelled` record was added.
- Outside Lands 2016 and Carnaval 2016 precede the lower boundary; they are excluded.
- Hardly Strictly 2026 is October 2-4, after the upper boundary, per the [organizer's dated FAQ](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/info-faq-2026/). It is excluded, even though research occurred in October.
- No separate canceled in-person Hardly Strictly 2020/2021 ranges were inferred. The verified virtual replacements represent those editions.
- No other in-window annual editions are omitted. Side events and the smaller Carnaval 2021 substitute are not part of this full-festival dataset.

## Source choices and corroboration

The JSON `source` points to evidence for the exact date range. Organizer archives are preferred when they expose dates; where an archive preserves performances but not date text, contemporary reporting or an event partner supplies dates instead. No uncited date was taken from a presumed annual pattern.

- Outside Lands: [organizer history and year-specific posters](https://sfoutsidelands.com/festival-history/) preserve the in-person editions and the 2020 exception. Older date ranges use KQED, JamBase, Guinness, or Pollstar where exact dates were readily available in text. The 2023 source is audio supplier Meyer Sound's retrospective. The 2024 source is the organizer's neighborhood map, and 2025 is its official ticket vendor. For 2021, the SFMTA notice is additionally checked against [Another Planet's October 29 event](https://apeconcerts.com/events/outside-lands-211029/) and the organizer's Halloween retrospective. [Eater's post-event 2026 report](https://sf.eater.com/scene-report/213324/outside-lands-2026-san-francisco-best-dishes) confirms August 7-9 actually occurred, supplementing the official FAQ.
- Carnaval: 2017 reporting explicitly says Saturday-Sunday May 27-28. The 2018 SFMTA bulletin, page 4 (PDF index 3), explicitly distinguishes the May 26-27 festival from the May 27 parade; street closures are not used as festival dates. KQED's 2019 and 2024 guides explicitly give both festival days. The 2022 map and 2025/2026 PDFs are organizer documents. The 2023 source is Calle 24, the local Latino cultural district. [KQED's May 2026 retrospective](https://www.kqed.org/news/12085109/carnaval-brings-colorful-costumes-and-latin-dancing-to-san-franciscos-mission-district) confirms the May 23-24 celebration occurred.
- Hardly Strictly: the [organizer archive index](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/) preserves annual editions. [2019](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2019/), [2022](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2022/), [2023](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2023/), [2024](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2024/), and [2025](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.com/archive/2025/) were accessible, but most omit date text. The 2016-2018 archive links and legacy year homepages returned fetch errors during research; published full lineups and schedules supply those dates. The 2019 date source is participating artists' label Nonesuch. The 2025 organizer schedule has separate pages for October 3, 4, and 5. [HSB TV's 2024 archive](https://hardlystrictlybluegrass.vhx.tv/hardly-strictly-bluegrass-2024-live-from-golden-gate-park) also preserves dated performances.

## Known source pitfalls

- The Wikipedia Outside Lands table displayed an erroneous August 8-11 range for 2025 in one search excerpt. The official ticket page says August 8-10; the latter is used.
- The SFMTA 2024 Hardly Strictly notice has a stray `2023` in its end-date sentence. Its daily schedule and independent 2024 schedule agree on October 4-6, 2024; the JSON uses the independent schedule URL instead.
- Current organizer festival/FAQ pages can roll forward to the next year. Year-specific PDFs, archived reporting, and the notes above provide more durable corroboration.
